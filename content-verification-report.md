# Content Verification Report
## Catholic Diocese of Tombura-Yambio Website

**Date:** October 7, 2026
**Purpose:** Identify content requiring diocesan verification before migration

---

## Executive Summary

This report identifies all content on the existing website that requires verification with the Diocese before migration to the new website. Verification is needed for:

- Inconsistent information across pages
- Outdated dates and references
- Potential changes since last update
- Missing or incomplete information
- Geopolitical changes (South Sudan state restructuring)

**Verification Needed:**

- Are the 7 counties still correct?

## Verification Process Recommended

### Step 1: Diocesan Review
- Bishop reviews critical information
- Curia reviews leadership information
- Department heads review their sections
- Deanery coordinators review parish information

### Step 2: Cross-Reference
- Compare with official diocesan documents
- Check against recent publications
- Verify with partner organizations
- Cross-check with other Catholic sources

### Step 3: Update
- Update outdated information
- Add missing information
- Correct inconsistencies
- Remove obsolete content

### Step 4: Approve
- Final approval from Bishop

- Sign-off on content accuracy
